Common Questions

How do the Smart Scales connect to the dashboard?

^

They connect via Wi-Fi instantly. Link them to your network using our app, and they start syncing data.

Does it work for both restaurant kitchens and retail stores?

^

Yes. You can configure scales to deduct ingredients based on recipes (kitchens) or track individual units for kits (retail).

Can I monitor weight, temperature, and humidity simultaneously?

^

Absolutely. Every scale is equipped with a load cell, a thermometer, and a hygrometer.

What happens if the internet goes down?

^

The scales have local memory. They store sensor data and automatically sync once the connection is restored.

How does the automated recipe deduction work?

^

When a dish is sold, the system calculates the expected weight drop on the scale and flags any discrepancies.
